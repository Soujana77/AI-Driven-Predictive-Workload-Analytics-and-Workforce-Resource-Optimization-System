# INDIVIDUAL ASSIGNMENT — MEMBER 4
# MANAGER DASHBOARD — FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT

You are Member 4 of the project:

**Agentic AI-Driven Workforce Capacity Forecasting and Resource Optimization System**

Your responsibility is to develop the complete Manager Dashboard frontend using React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS.

You own the manager-facing user interface. You do not own the central backend, forecasting model, optimization engine or Employee Dashboard.

## 1. Your objective

Build a complete, professional manager workspace through which a software-company manager can:

- View workforce information.
- Manage employees and teams.
- Manage projects and tasks.
- Understand historical workload.
- View future workload forecasts.
- Compare workload with capacity.
- Review resource optimization recommendations.
- Monitor workforce risks.
- Interact with the AI assistant.
- Approve or reject proposed actions.
- Generate workforce reports.
- Configure planning preferences.

This must be a working frontend connected to documented APIs, not merely a static design.

## 2. Mandatory design system

This design is fixed and must not be changed.

### Main page background

Use a Champagne-to-Vanilla gradient across the entire Manager Dashboard:

- Champagne: `#F8E7C9`
- Vanilla: `#FFF4D6`

The background must remain a gradient, not a flat color.

Use the gradient on the overall application background and maintain its appearance while navigating between manager pages.

### Other colors

- Emerald Ink: `#064E3B` — sidebar, branding and primary navigation.
- Burnt Orange: `#FC6C26` — primary actions, active navigation, highlights and accents.
- Vanilla and warm cream — cards and content surfaces.
- Dark charcoal/deep green — text.

### Visual style

- Modern enterprise SaaS dashboard.
- Professional and clean.
- Consistent typography.
- Rounded cards with subtle shadows.
- Clear charts and data tables.
- Consistent buttons, forms, filters and spacing.
- Responsive layouts.
- Accessible contrast.

Do not introduce a competing theme or plain white/grey page background.
